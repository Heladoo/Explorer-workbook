Draw a single sheet of many small black-and-white doodle icons for a
children's activity book — like a hand-drawn sticker sheet, scattered loosely
across the page rather than lined up in a strict grid.

Local flavor: Kfar Hanokdim

Draw one clearly separated icon for each of the following subjects, evenly
scattered with generous white space around every one so each can be cropped
out on its own later:
• the camel yard
• the Nokdim valley lookout
• the Dead Sea shore
• the date palm grove
• desert lizards
• Nubian ibex
• camels
• goats and sheep
• desert wildflowers after rain
• white broom shrubs
• date palms
• tamarisk bushes
• riding a camel
• sitting around the campfire
• stargazing after dark
• a donkey ride
• dates from the palm trees

• cardamom coffee

Style:
• Bold, clean, uniform black outlines on white with large open areas to color — no shading, no hatching, no grey fills, no solid black areas
• Friendly modern children's picture-book line art: rounded shapes, even line weight
• Each icon simple enough for a 5-7 year old to recognise instantly
• Every icon roughly the same size, at arm's length from its neighbours — nothing overlapping or touching another icon
• Gentle and welcoming, nothing frightening, no weapons, no injuries

Constraints:
• Portrait A4 (210 x 297 mm), 300 DPI, pure white background
• No grid lines, boxes, dividers or borders around individual icons — just the icons themselves, loose on the white page
• No text, letters, numbers, captions, labels, speech bubbles, watermarks or logos anywhere in the image
• Prints cleanly in black and white on a home printer

This sheet doubles as a coloring page as printed, and each icon can later be
cropped into its own individual symbol drawing.
