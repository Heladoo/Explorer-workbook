Draw one simple black-and-white icon for a children's activity book.

Subject:
a bicycle seen from the side, both wheels fully visible

Layout:
A single centred object filling most of the square, seen from the one angle that
makes it most recognisable. Nothing else in the picture — no background, no
scenery, no ground line, no shadow, no frame, no border, no checkbox.

Style:
• Bold, clean, uniform black outlines on white with large open areas to color — no shading, no hatching, no grey fills, no solid black areas
• Friendly modern children's picture-book line art: rounded shapes, even line weight
• One object only, drawn simply enough for a 5-9 year old to recognise it instantly
• Gentle and welcoming, nothing frightening, no weapons, no injuries

Constraints:
• Square image, 1:1, on a pure white background
• No text, letters, numbers, captions, labels, speech bubbles, watermarks or logos anywhere in the image
• Stays clearly readable when printed small, about 25 mm across
• Prints cleanly in black and white on a home printer
